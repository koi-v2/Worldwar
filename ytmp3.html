Why did conversion fail?

The processing service may be offline, the link may not be supported, or the server may reject the request. Check the error shown in the converter and try again later.

Which audio quality should I choose?

128 kbps generally creates smaller files. 192 or 256 kbps offers a balance, while 320 kbps creates larger files. Higher bitrate cannot restore detail absent from the source.
